Simple process

From listing to handshake in four steps

  1. 1

    Post or browse

    Sellers list with photos and details. Buyers search nearby used bikes by brand, price, and mileage.

  2. 2

    Message directly

    Chat with the owner — no broker in the middle, no hidden fees, no awkward phone calls through a third party.

  3. 3

    Meet & inspect

    Visit at a time that works for both of you. Check the bike in person before money changes hands.

  4. 4

    Close the deal

    Agree on a fair price, hand over the keys, and ride away — person to person.
